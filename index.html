I lead technical teams and build the systems myself. I was selected as incoming President of Cal Poly CSAI — the largest computing organization on campus, with 1,000+ members — after a year as Director of Research, running the club's portfolio across four teams and launching both the CSAI Journal of Computer Science and a $1,000 scholarship hackathon.

The technical range underneath that is deliberately broad: computer vision and embedded inference, transformer language models trained from scratch, recommendation systems, and the full-stack web and mobile products they ship inside. I've owned product vision and success criteria for a four-phase research program, run four teams on an Agile cadence, and shipped software to production for real users at the Surfrider Foundation.

The part I care about is the space between a model and a decision — working out what a system actually needs to do, proving that it does it, and getting it in front of people.
